Transglobal Food Tech is a food, nutrition and wellness holding company that connects farmers, natural ingredients, innovation and consumers.

Our Vision

To build a global portfolio of food, nutrition, wellness and natural-product brands that creates value from farm to consumer.

Our Mission

To source quality ingredients, work closely with farmers and develop innovative products that bring together nature, nutrition, science, technology and convenience.

Our Purpose

To make better nutrition part of everyday life — supporting farmers and creating healthier, more sustainable choices for people everywhere.

From Farm to Future

We believe farmers should be an integral part of the value chain. We work directly with farmers and source their produce, helping create sustainable opportunities for them.

Our aim is to reduce agricultural wastage, increase value from their produce and connect local farms to national and international markets.

Entrepreneurship. Impact. A Shared Vision.

Visha Michelle Iyer

Co-Founder

Entrepreneur and creative thinker who has worked with NGOs, farmers and government initiatives. Her interest in food processing grew from seeing farmers lose significant value when fresh produce was discarded because it could not reach markets economically. Her training in food dehydration led her to explore ways of preserving agricultural produce, reducing wastage and creating greater value from what farmers grow.

Aditya Mahimkar

Co-Founder

Entrepreneur and social-impact advocate who has worked extensively with NGOs, youth and farming communities. Through his own NGO and various initiatives, he has worked with people across communities, with a particular focus on creating opportunities and improving livelihoods.
