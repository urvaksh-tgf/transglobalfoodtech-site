Four ways to tell the story

Each one is the complete site. Nothing here is public or indexed; the live site is unchanged.

  • 1The enchanted field

    One 3D landscape behind the whole site. You walk through it from dawn to night as you scroll.

  • 2The storybook

    A fable told in chapters, with painted illustrations and pages that turn in.

  • 3The global house

    The gold globe, the dive into India and a polished corporate finish.

  • 4The harvest party

    A crowd of 3D fruit and spice characters that dance and react to you, with a loud sticker style.
